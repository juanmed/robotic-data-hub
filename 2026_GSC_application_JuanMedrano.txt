2.Can you describe your business model ?
*
GamiphyAI is a **Korea-first robotics automation company** that monetizes paid SME deployments to build a defensible robotics data and skill platform, with a unique fourth revenue pillar targeting product brands. We are not building an open marketplace on day one; we run a services-to-platform sequence that avoids the cold-start problem typical of two-sided industrial markets.

**Four-pillar revenue model:**

1. **Robot Consumers (SMEs)** pay for paid PoCs, deployments, and recurring support/subscription on high-mix, low-volume (HMLV) workflows — packing, kitting, labeling, light assembly, small-parts handling, food prep.
2. **Robot Creators** (labs, data teams) earn by supplying validated task data, skills, and algorithms tied to real customer KPIs.
3. **Platform** captures transaction and licensing fees on validated skills, datasets, and benchmarks.
4. **Product Brands / OEMs** pay for **Brand Dataset Services** — ensuring their appliances, tools, components, and equipment are represented in robot training datasets so their products remain compatible with AI-powered robots ("brand insurance" against dataset-driven obsolescence).

**Korea pricing architecture (automation wedge):**

| Item | Price |
|---|---|
| Paid PoC (2–4 weeks, KPI baseline, go/no-go) | KRW 8M–15M |
| Deployment / activation (line or workcell) | KRW 12M–25M |
| Recurring software / data / skill subscription | KRW 1.0M–2.0M / month |
| Remote support SLA (on-site billed separately) | KRW 0.6M–1.2M / month |

**Illustrative 24-month customer value (Korea, automation):** PoC KRW 10M + Deployment KRW 18M + 24 × KRW 2.3M recurring (KRW 55.2M) + hardware coordination KRW 1M = **KRW 84.2M per converted customer**.

**Brand Dataset Services pricing architecture (subscription + variable model).** Brand services are priced against competitive necessity rather than operational ROI payback. The buyers are VP Product, CMO, and Chief Innovation Officer — not factory operations — and they compare our price against the cost of custom integration engineering per robot platform (USD 50K–200K per integration), lost market access due to incompatibility, delayed time-to-market, and competitive disadvantage versus better-represented brands. From that reference, even USD 500K/year is a rounding error against traditional market-access spend.

Competitive landscape in the Korean market

**Direct Korean competitor — Marosol (en.myrobotsolution.com), operated by Bigwave Robotics.**

Marosol positions itself as a "Total Robot Solution Provider" and operates primarily as a **robot e-commerce marketplace**. It catalogs eight categories — industrial, collaborative, logistics, cleaning, guide, delivery, humanoid, serving robots — plus peripherals, grippers, and ROS-compatible robots, connecting third-party robot vendors with Korean customers. Its stated services are product sales, integration services, and expert consultation, with the value proposition "robotics automation made easy" via a centralized marketplace.

**How GamiphyAI differs from Marosol:**

| Dimension | Marosol | GamiphyAI |
| **Core business** | Robot e-commerce / reseller marketplace | Workflow-specific paid PoC → deployment → recurring software/data/skill subscription |
| **Unit of value sold** | Physical robot SKUs | Completion of a named SME workflow against KPIs |
| **Revenue quality** | Hardware resale (thin, one-time margins) | Recurring subscription + high-margin data/skills + brand services |
| **Data asset** | None | Proprietary task-outcome datasets and skill packages per workflow |
| **Defensibility** | Distribution breadth | Deployment playbooks, benchmarks, rights-managed skills, brand network effects |
| **Brand services** | None | Brand Dataset Services — a pillar Marosol does not address |
| **Liability posture** | Intermediary, limited direct seller liability | Direct PoC and deployment responsibility with explicit acceptance criteria |

In short, Marosol is a **horizontal robot distribution channel** and GamiphyAI is a **vertical workflow automation and data platform**. The two are not substitutes — in fact, Marosol or a similar reseller could become a procurement/distribution partner for the hardware side of our deployments, while we own the workflow, data, skills, recurring subscription, and brand services.

**Other competitors referenced in the GamiphyAI business plan** (not Korean-headquartered, but overlapping in target space):

- **Traditional fixed automation integrators** (custom cells, conveyors, PLC/vision): high reliability on stable high-volume lines, but high capex, long cycles, and poor fit for SME high-mix work. GamiphyAI edge: 1-day installs, ≥10× lower upfront integration fees, flexibility for non-precision tasks.
- **ExchAInge** (`exchainge.net`): generic "marketplace for verified physical AI datasets" focused on dataset monetization. Does not start from specific SME workloads or KPIs, lacks deployment responsibility. GamiphyAI edge: dataset marketplace is tied to real SME PoCs, continuously refined through deployments.
- **RobotDataMarket** (`robotdatamarket.com`): positions as "marketplace for robotics training data" but datasets are "coming soon." Marketplace-only, no closed loop with real automation outcomes. GamiphyAI edge: validation through real SME PoCs creating a repeatable supply-unlocks-demand loop.
- **SignIQ Lab** (`signiq-lab.ai`): full-stack data engine / teleoperation service for sim-to-real transfer. Geared to large robotics teams, higher price points, longer cycles. Not optimized for Korean SMEs. GamiphyAI edge: Korea-first SME focus, small datasets, rapid PoC-to-deployment with ongoing monthly support.
- **Sensei** (`senseirobotics.com`): "Scale AI for robotics data," low-cost collection hardware plus human operator network. Primarily solves data supply for robotics teams, not deployment/operations for SME production lines. GamiphyAI edge: combined SME need discovery + PoC + deployment + maintenance + creator marketplace tied to validated use cases.

**Net competitive position:** no Korean-headquartered competitor today combines (a) SME workflow PoCs, (b) a data-and-skill platform, and (c) brand dataset services. GamiphyAI's closest structural competitor in Korea (Marosol) operates in a different layer of the stack entirely.
