### Problem & Product: What is your startup building? What problem are you solving? Please describe your product and what it solves in simple terms

Korea alone has 500K+ manufacturing businesses; over 7M exist globally. Most can't afford automation: robots are expensive, rigid and unsafe. SME manufacturers face labor shortages, quality issues, missed deadlines and lost revenue. GamiphyAI provides a platform based on physical AI: safe robots for high-mix, low-volume tasks, and a matching marketplace of tasks where each deployment generates reusable robot skills. Reliable, predictable and flexible automation designed from a desktop.
